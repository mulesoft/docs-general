= Mule Credits Usage and Rates

A Mule Credit is a fungible currency that allows you to draw down from a single capacity pool as you consume services across MuleSoft. Instead of managing different usage types for all products, you purchase a package of credits that can be used flexibly across the entire MuleSoft platform. As you use these capabilities, Anypoint Platform deducts the corresponding credit amounts from your available balance.

Credits are shared across your entire Anypoint Platform organization and cannot be managed per business group. Use Mule Credits before the Order End Date set in the Usage Details table on your Order Form — rollovers are not permitted. To see which usage types are included in your subscription, refer to your Order Form, Subscription Plan, or Features and Entitlement Document.

== Usage Types

Each usage type (also referred to as a meter) tracks a specific dimension of platform consumption. Credit rates vary by monthly consumption volume and reset on the first day of each calendar month. You consume credits at the same rate across all environments, including production, pre-production, sandbox, and design. For the full tiered rate table, see the <<_rate_cards,Rate Cards>> section.

[%header%autowidth.spread]
|===
| Usage Type | Description

| *MuleSoft Flows*
| A flow is a sequence of logical operations within a deployed Mule application that contains a Mule event source or APIkit route. Anypoint Platform tracks the number of flows across all business groups, environments, and applications on an hourly basis. For every 24-hour period starting at 00:00 UTC, the hour with the highest flow count is identified as the daily peak. Based on your contract term, MuleSoft aggregates these daily peaks over the month or year to determine your final usage. This is a sum-based meter — unlike the previous high-watermark model, all daily flow counts contribute to your total usage. Subflows used for error handling or modular processing are included in the count.

| *MuleSoft Messages*
| The total volume of data containers processed by the runtime. The platform counts a message each time an event source triggers it, and aggregates message counts daily (UTC time) and monthly. Changes to a message as it moves through application flows are not tracked — only the initial trigger is counted.

| *Managed Assets*
| An asset is considered managed if it has at least one active instance in https://docs.mulesoft.com/api-manager/latest/latest-overview-concept[API Manager^] deployed to a https://docs.mulesoft.com/mule-gateway/mule-gateway-capabilities-mule4[Mule Gateway^] or https://docs.mulesoft.com/gateway/latest/[Omni Gateway^]. Managed assets include APIs, Agents, Model Context Protocol (MCP) Servers, and Large Language Models (LLMs). Usage is counted per business group per day; if an asset has active instances across multiple business groups, it counts as one asset per group. Multiple instances within the same business group do not incur extra charges. Anypoint Platform deducts credits as long as the asset status is Active in API Manager, even if there are no calls to the asset.

| *Gateway Requests*
| Any request received by https://docs.mulesoft.com/gateway/latest/[Omni Gateway^]. Usage is measured as the sum of all processed requests, regardless of response status.

| *IDP Pages*
| The number of individual document pages processed by Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) in https://docs.mulesoft.com/idp/[MuleSoft IDP^] or https://blogs.mulesoft.com/news/mulesoft-for-flow-intelligent-document-processing/[MuleSoft for Flow: IDP^]. Each page within a document is counted as a separate unit, and only successful LLM responses are charged.

| *Flow Integration Tasks*
| Individual units of work or actions (reading, creating, or updating a record) executed within https://www.mulesoft.com/platform/flow-integration[MuleSoft for Flow: Integration^].

| *Private Spaces*
| A https://docs.mulesoft.com/cloudhub-2/ch2-private-space-about[Private Space^] is a dedicated, isolated environment in CloudHub 2.0 for running your apps. Credits are consumed based on the total number of provisioned Private Spaces per day. After you deprovision a Private Space, it stops consuming credits from the next calendar date onwards.

| *Network Connections*
| Network connections allow you to securely connect corporate data centers and on-premises applications to CloudHub 1.0 and https://docs.mulesoft.com/cloudhub-2/ch2-private-space-about#private-network[CloudHub 2.0^] using a VPN or AWS Transit Gateway. Credits are consumed based on the total number of active network connections per day. After you deprovision a network connection, it stops consuming credits from the next calendar date onwards.

| *Load Balancers*
| https://docs.mulesoft.com/cloudhub/cloudhub-dedicated-load-balancer[Dedicated Load Balancers^] used to distribute incoming traffic across multiple workers or instances. Credits are consumed based on the total number of provisioned load balancers per day. After you deprovision a load balancer, it stops consuming credits from the next calendar date onwards.
|===

== Rate Cards

You consume Mule Credits at the applicable multiplier rate based on the usage type identified in the https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/mulesoft/Mule_Credit_Rate_Card.pdf[Mule Credit Rate Card^].

Mule Credits must be used before the Order End Date set forth in the Usage Details table on the Order Form, and no rollover will be permitted. If your usage happens to exceed your contracted amount, MuleSoft automatically sends a monthly overage invoice to your primary billing contact at your standard contracted rate. Contact your account team with any questions about your contract or billing.

== Viewing Usage and Billing

Mule Credits usage, monthly statements, and overage billing are centralized in Salesforce Digital Wallet. If you have an Anypoint Platform organization, you can also view your usage and entitlement details in Anypoint Usage Reports for cross-platform transparency.

To view your Mule Credits consumption:

. From the *App Launcher*, click *Digital Wallet*.
. Locate the *Mule Credits* consumption card in the *Consumption Cards* tab.

Digital Wallet is available in Lightning Experience in Enterprise and Unlimited editions, and requires the View Consumption user permission.

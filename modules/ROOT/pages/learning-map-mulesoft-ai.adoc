= Getting Started with MuleSoft AI
:page-article-style: learning-map

MuleSoft is leading the industry in agentic transformation, moving organizations beyond traditional digital transformation to achieve enhanced actionability through an advanced suite of AI products and features. This transition is supported by key capabilities designed for the development, orchestration, management, governance, deployment, and observability of AI agents. MuleSoft aims to convert disparate agents into a cohesive, trusted, and actionable network, leveraging existing deterministic investments for non-deterministic outcomes.

The end-to-end AI journey for MuleSoft AI consists of various tasks, each with links to relevant content to assist you in completing them.

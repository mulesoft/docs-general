= MuleSoft for AI
:page-article-style: learning-map

MuleSoft for AI empowers agent action with flexible intelligent tooling that integrates systems and automates processes with AI to help accelerate your ability to deliver an actionable, connected enterprise by helping you build, orchestrate, and manage AI-driven experiences. 

The end-to-end AI journey for MuleSoft AI consists of various tasks, each with links to relevant content to assist you in completing them.
